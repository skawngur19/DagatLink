Demo script

  1. Turn the signal off, then tap I'm safe and save a catch — both wait on the phone.
  2. Turn the signal on — the queue uploads and the dashboard updates.
  3. On the dashboard, press +60m twice to walk a trip past its expected return.
  4. Publish a red advisory — the phone receives it and reads it aloud.

Notes

  • Voice uses the browser's Filipino voice. Hiligaynon prompt text is shown and spoken with the closest available voice; production replaces it with recorded audio.
  • Your answers are stored in this browser only.
